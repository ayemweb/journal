Author Guidelines

The journal publishes half-yearly, with submissions accepted year-round for the next available issue.

Manuscript template

Prepare your manuscript using the official template: Download manuscript template (.docx). It sets out the required sections, headings, citation style and formatting.

Manuscript requirements

  • Submit original work that is not under review elsewhere.
  • Include a title, author names and affiliations, abstract, keywords, main text and references.
  • Submit the manuscript as a Word (.docx) file.
  • Follow the journal formatting template when it is released.

Publication fee

There is no fee to submit or during review. A publication fee applies only once a manuscript is accepted, and is confirmed with authors at that time. See the fees and refunds policy.

How to submit

Send your manuscript to profdrayem64@gmail.com. See the submission page.

After submission

Manuscripts are screened by the editorial office and then sent for peer review. Authors are informed of the decision by email.
