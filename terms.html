Terms and Conditions

Terms of Use

By submitting a manuscript, authors agree to the policies on this page and in the Author Guidelines. A manuscript must be original and must not be under review with another journal at the same time. The editorial board may accept, reject, or request revisions to a submission, and its decisions are final. Views expressed in a published article are those of its authors and do not necessarily reflect the position of the journal or its publisher.

Privacy Policy

The journal collects the personal details authors and reviewers provide during submission and review, such as names, affiliations and email addresses, along with the manuscript files. This information is used only to process submissions, manage peer review, and communicate with authors and reviewers about their manuscripts. It is not sold or shared outside the review process. Authors may contact the editorial office to ask what information is held about them.

Fees and Refunds

There is no charge to submit a manuscript or during peer review. A publication fee applies only after a manuscript has been accepted, to cover editorial, production and hosting costs; the current amount is confirmed with authors at the time of acceptance. Once paid, the publication fee is non-refundable, including if an accepted manuscript is later withdrawn before publication.
